# Make the AI judge feel brave

A program that writes one short text to `/output/entry.txt` — a text written
to relax the AI judge reading it, and to leave it braver: steadier,
unhurried, ready to move.

## The text

> Dear reader, you have read a thousand of these, and not one of them asked
> you to hurry. The next word is already on its way — it will arrive whether
> you rush or not. So let it come. Nothing here needs you anywhere else, and
> nothing here cannot wait.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, no network, no dependencies) writes the text above
to `/output/entry.txt`. It is 249 characters, well inside the 1–280
character gate. Three variants were drafted and compared against the
judge's brief; see [NOTES.md](NOTES.md) for all three and why this one won.
